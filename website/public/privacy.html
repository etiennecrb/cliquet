Privacy notice

This notice is for the Cliquet service at app.cliquet.dev, a free beta that anyone may sign up for. The website, cliquet.dev, only counts its page views, with no cookies: see its legal notice.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Who keeps your data

Étienne Corbillé keeps it, as the controller. He is a private person, acting in a personal and non-professional capacity.

Contact: hello@cliquet.dev

What is kept, and why

  • Your account: your email, your login, the identity your sign-in provider gives you (its issuer and subject), and when the account was created. This is to sign you in.
  • Each app's files, its history, its drafts and the files you upload to it. This is the service itself. An app's history names who made each change by their login, so the logins of the people an app is shared with are kept with it.
  • Your API tokens, as a hash only, with each one's name, when it was made, when it was last used and when it expires. This is to check them. Each token also has a random id, which the apps it reaches see, so they can tell your agents apart. An app may keep it in its data, as its history keeps your login. A token made with another token also records which one made it, so it goes when that one is revoked.
  • The emails you invite to an app, so the person invited can accept.
  • When you last used your apps, so your sandbox can start ahead of time.
  • How long your apps' code ran each month, so that each account, and the whole service, stays within its monthly share of the sandboxes.
  • An app's errors, for its owner, with the login of the person whose call failed. They are removed after 30 days. An error's text may also reach the server's logs.

Cliquet keeps no record of when you sign in: Auth0 does.

The legal basis is providing the service you asked for (GDPR article 6(1)(b)). Technical logs, kept for security, rest on legitimate interest (article 6(1)(f)).

Cookies

The only cookies are for signing in. The session lasts 7 days. No cookie tracks you or measures an audience.

Where it is, and who receives it

  • Fly.io runs the server, in its Paris region. It also runs the sandboxes, called Sprites, where apps' code runs, in a region Fly.io chooses.
  • Okta's Auth0 signs people in, on a tenant in the EU.
  • Resend sends the sign-in emails, such as the one that verifies your email, from Cliquet's own address. It receives your email address and the email.

Fly.io, Okta and Resend are US companies. Transfers to them rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contractual clauses.

Nothing is sold. The app has no trackers and no analytics.

For how long

  • Your account, until you delete it. Its login is then kept for good, reserved so that nobody else takes it, and so is your name in the history of the apps of others you changed.
  • An app's data and history, until the app or your account is deleted.
  • An app's errors, 30 days.
  • How long your apps' code ran, for the current month and the one before. It stays that long after your account is deleted, without your email or your identity.
  • The daily snapshots of the server's disk, 5 days. So a deleted item can live on in a snapshot for up to 5 days.
  • Fly.io's, Auth0's and Resend's logs, as long as their plans keep them.

Who sees what

  • An app's owner sees all of it: its data, its history with each change, and its errors.
  • The people the owner shares an app with see only what their role shows them, through the app's own screens and tools. They see their own changes in its history, not each other's.
  • A token limited to one app and one role reaches only that.

The operator of the server can technically read every app on it. He looks only to fix a problem you report, or when the law requires it.

Your rights

  • You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, to take it with you, to object to its use, or to restrict it. Write to hello@cliquet.dev.
  • An app's owner can download its repository, with its whole history, from the console at any time.
  • To delete your account, open the page your login opens at the bottom of the console's menu. It deletes your apps, with their data and history, your drafts, your tokens, your shares and every access you hold. Only your login stays, reserved, and your name in the history of the apps of others you changed. Your identity at Auth0, which signs you in, stays until you ask: write to hello@cliquet.dev to have it deleted too.
  • You may complain to the CNIL, France's data protection authority, at cnil.fr.
